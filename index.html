About Me

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Thapar Institute of Information and Technology, maintaining a 9.15 CGPA. My work spans the full spectrum of modern engineering — writing Python ML pipelines and TensorFlow/Keras models, programming Arduino-based mechatronic systems in embedded C, and deploying interactive web apps with Streamlit.

I'm drawn to projects that fuse hardware intuition with software intelligence — whether that's detecting surface cracks in steel at ~99% accuracy, building an AI botanist that speaks its findings aloud, or programming a catapult to measure its own angular velocity in real time. I care about making technology tangible and useful.
