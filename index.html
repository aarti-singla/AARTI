About Me

I'm an Electronics & Computer Engineering student at Thapar Institute of Information and Technology, currently maintaining a 9.15 CGPA. My interests sit at the overlap of AI/ML, computer vision, and embedded electronics — designing systems that can perceive the physical world and act on it intelligently.

I've built a plant-diagnosing AI that speaks its findings aloud, trained a CNN to spot cracks in steel with near-perfect accuracy, and programmed a mechatronic catapult to measure its own angular velocity in real time. I'm drawn to projects where hardware meets intelligence.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm tutoring students, volunteering, and yes — I once placed 3rd nationally in a timekeeping championship. Counting seconds is a competitive sport.
